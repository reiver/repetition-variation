Repetition Variation

say one thing hundreds of different ways

by Charles Iliya Krempeaux

Repetition Variation is a marketing strategy where the same thing is expressed multiples times in multiple ways, and sometimes in multiple formats.

Why

Why do some people and organizations engage in repetition variation‽ Here are some reasons:

Audience Fragmentation

The audience you may want to reach may not all be in the same place at the same time.

For example, some may watch short-form videos on TikTok, or Loops. Others may only watch long-form videos YouTube, or PeerTube. Yet others may on listen to podcasts. Some may only read (physical) print, such as (physical) magazines and (physical) newspapers. Yet others may only read e-mail newsletters. Some may only read carousels on Instagram, and Pixelfed. Etc.

To reach as many people from your target audience, a the same core message or core story may need to be "repackaged" for each for of these different media.

Also, there different demographics and segments on the same platform may need a different message. For example, what the "package" that computer science university students respond to may be different than the "package" 50 year old executives respond to.

Repetition and Retention
Economics of Scale

Doing original, high-cost deep-dive analysis or investigative reporting tends to be expensive, and is often time consuming.

To both maximize the ROI of either of those and to be able to publish content more often, re-telling the same message from either of these over and over again, as (for example) opinion pieces, summary threads, video explainers, social media clips, etc, can help.
